Human Anatomy Laboratory

Interactive 3D stylized human body with transparent torso. Click on the Liver, Heart, or Lungs to highlight and inspect physiological functions.

⚡
Physics Kinematics Lab

Ballistic projectile simulation. Adjust launch angle θ, speed v₀, and planetary gravity (Earth, Moon, Mars). Live telemetry plots parabolic paths.

🧪
Chemistry Substance Lab

Choose Substance A (HCl, Water, Vinegar, CuSO₄) and Substance B (NaOH, Baking Soda, Potassium, Ammonia, Zinc). Click Mix / React to trigger real-time color changes, effervescence, or metal explosions!

Multimodal "Snap & Ask"

Click "Snap 3D & Ask AI" to capture the exact canvas frame. Gemini analyzes both your screenshot and simulation telemetry.
